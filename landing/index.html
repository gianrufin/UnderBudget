Built for a cart in one hand, a phone in the other

Designed for the store, not the desk

UnderBudget is designed to be used while you're actually shopping, not reviewed later at a desk. The keypad stays docked within thumb's reach. Buttons are sized for quick taps, not careful aim. The layout accounts for your phone's screen edges, so nothing important sits where your thumb already is.

It works in bright store lighting and dim ones, in light mode or a true dark mode. And because it installs like an app, opening it takes the same one tap as opening your calculator.
